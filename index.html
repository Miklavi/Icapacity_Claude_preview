All our NDIS supports.

We're registered with the NDIS Commission for 13 support groups. Here is what they mean for you.

Everyday support

  • Personal careHelp with showering, dressing, eating and getting ready for the day.
  • Help around the houseCleaning, laundry and gardening.
  • Shared living supportDaily help when you live with others.

Skills and community

  • Life skillsCooking, budgeting, travel and everyday independence.
  • Getting out and aboutSocial, sport and community activities.
  • Group and centre activitiesActivities with other people, in a group or at a centre.
  • Creative community programsNew ways to take part in community life.

Home and living

  • Supported Independent Living (SIL)Help at home so you can live as independently as possible.
  • Specialist Disability Accommodation (SDA)Housing designed for people with very high support needs.
  • Finding and keeping a homeHelp to find a place to live and manage a tenancy.

Planning and therapy

  • Support CoordinationHelp to understand and use your NDIS plan.
  • Life changesLeaving hospital, moving home, starting school or work.
  • Social Work and therapy supportsSomeone to think problems through with you.

How it works.

  1. Call us or leave your number

    It takes two minutes. No documents needed.

  2. We talk it through

    We listen and answer your questions, in your preferred language where we can. We call you back by the next business day.

  3. We meet and plan together

    We agree what support you need, when, and how it's funded. You get a clear service agreement before anything starts.

  4. Support starts

    With a worker matched to your needs, language and personality. You can meet them first, free of charge, and we'll change your worker if it isn't the right fit.

What happens on the first call?

We ask a few simple questions: who the support is for, what you need help with, and which language you prefer. You don't need your plan or any documents. You can just ask questions.

Common questions.

Do I need an NDIS plan to use your services?

To use NDIS funding for our services you generally need an NDIS plan. You don't need one to talk to us. Call and we'll explain.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

What if I don't have a plan yet? Can you help me start?

We can explain the first steps and point you to the NDIS website. Whether you get a plan is the NDIS's decision, and we can't promise an outcome.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

How much does it cost? Are there extra fees?

NDIS prices follow the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits. Your service agreement shows the rates before support starts.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

How quickly can support start?

It depends on what you need and who is available. We'll give you an honest answer on the first call.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

Will I have the same worker each time?

We aim to keep a small, steady team with you.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

Which areas do you cover?

We provide services across Australia. Call us to check what we can arrange in your area.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

What happens if a worker can't come?

We arrange a replacement worker and tell you as early as we can.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

What if I need to cancel a visit?

Cancellations follow the NDIS rules in the Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits. We explain them before support starts.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

You see a clear service agreement before anything starts, and you can end it with the notice set out in that agreement.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.

What's the difference between Support Coordination and Social Work?

Support Coordination helps you understand and use your NDIS plan. Social Work helps you work through a complicated situation and make decisions. Some people use one, some use both.

Still unsure? Call 0428 403 859.
